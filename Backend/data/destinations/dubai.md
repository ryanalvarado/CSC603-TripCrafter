# Dubai, UAE

Dubai is spread along the coastline with key areas: Downtown Dubai (Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall), Dubai Marina (waterfront dining, beach), Old Dubai (Deira, Gold Souk, Spice Souk), and Palm Jumeirah (luxury resorts). JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence) is popular for beach access.

The Dubai Metro is modern and efficient with two lines (Red and Green). A Nol Silver card costs AED 25 and can be topped up. Taxis are affordable and metered. The Dubai Tram connects Marina to JBR. Water taxis (abra) cross the Creek for AED 1 — a must-do experience.

Dubai follows Islamic customs. Public displays of affection are frowned upon. Dress modestly outside beach and pool areas — cover shoulders and knees when visiting malls, mosques, and public spaces. Alcohol is only served in licensed venues (hotels and select restaurants). Friday is the holy day; many businesses open later.

November through March is the best time to visit with temperatures around 25-30°C. Summer (June-September) regularly exceeds 45°C and outdoor activities become impractical. Ramadan affects restaurant hours and public behavior.

Street food in Old Dubai (shawarma, falafel) costs AED 10-20. Mid-range restaurants average AED 80-150 per person. The Dubai Mall food court offers international options for AED 30-50. Brunch culture is huge — Friday brunches at hotels are a social event (AED 200-500+).
