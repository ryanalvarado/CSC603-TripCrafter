# Maldives

The Maldives consists of 1,192 coral islands grouped into 26 atolls. Tourism centers on resort islands (one resort per island), guesthouses on local islands, and liveaboard boats. Malé is the capital and main transit hub. Velana International Airport (MLE) is the entry point.

Transport between islands is by speedboat (30-90 minutes, $150-400 round trip) or seaplane ($300-600 round trip) depending on distance. Domestic flights connect distant atolls. Book transfers through your resort — independent island-hopping requires planning.

The Maldives is an Islamic country. On resort islands, Western dress and alcohol are permitted. On local (inhabited) islands, modest dress is required — cover shoulders and knees. Bikinis are only appropriate on designated "bikini beaches" on local islands, not on regular beaches.

Dry season (November-April) is peak season with the best visibility for snorkeling and diving. The southwest monsoon (May-October) brings rain and rougher seas but lower prices and excellent surfing conditions.

Resorts operate on all-inclusive or half-board plans. A meal at a resort can cost $50-100+ per person. Local island guesthouses offer meals for $10-20. Water and imported goods are expensive throughout the Maldives. Bring reef-safe sunscreen — the coral reefs are protected.
