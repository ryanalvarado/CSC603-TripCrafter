# Sydney, Australia

Sydney's main areas include Circular Quay (Opera House, Harbour Bridge), The Rocks (historic district), Darling Harbour (family attractions), Bondi Beach (iconic surf beach), and Surry Hills/Newtown (food and nightlife). The CBD is compact and walkable. The Northern Beaches (Manly) are accessible by ferry.

The Opal card works on trains, buses, ferries, and light rail. Tap on and off with a contactless card or Opal card. The ferry from Circular Quay to Manly (30 minutes) is one of the best-value scenic rides in the world. Trains connect the airport to the CBD in 15 minutes.

Australians are casual and direct. Tipping is not expected but appreciated (10% at nicer restaurants). Sunscreen is essential year-round — UV levels are extreme even on cloudy days. Swim between the red and yellow flags at patrolled beaches only.

Summer (December-February) is peak season with hot weather (25-35°C) and busy beaches. Autumn (March-May) is ideal with warm days and fewer crowds. Winter (June-August) is mild (10-18°C). Sydney's ocean water is comfortable for swimming October through April.

Fish and chips at Bondi cost AUD 15-20. A meal at a mid-range restaurant is AUD 25-40. The Sydney Fish Market is excellent for fresh seafood. Weekend markets (Paddington, Glebe, Rozelle) offer diverse food stalls for AUD 10-18.
