# Santorini, Greece

Santorini's main towns line the caldera rim: Fira (capital, commercial center), Oia (famous sunsets, luxury hotels), Imerovigli (highest point, quietest), and Firostefani (between Fira and Imerovigli). The caldera path connects these towns on foot (Fira to Oia is about 10 km, 3-4 hours). Kamari and Perissa are the main beach towns on the east coast.

Public buses connect Fira to most villages and beaches. ATV/quad rentals are popular but roads are narrow and accidents are common. Taxis are limited — book through your hotel. The port (Athinios) is connected to Fira by bus or taxi. Ferries from Athens (Piraeus) take 5-8 hours; flights take 45 minutes.

Greek hospitality is warm and personal. A simple "Kalimera" (good morning) or "Efcharistó" (thank you) goes a long way. Tipping 5-10% is appreciated but not mandatory. Water at restaurants is not free — you'll be charged for bottled water.

Peak season is July-August with extreme crowds and heat (35°C+). Shoulder season (May-June, September-October) is ideal with warm weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices. Most hotels and restaurants close November through March.

Dining in Oia with caldera views is expensive (€30-60 per person). Fira has more affordable options (€15-25). Gyros and souvlaki from street shops cost €3-5. The local wine (Assyrtiko) is excellent and unique to the volcanic terroir.
