# London, England

London is divided into boroughs and neighborhoods. Central areas include Westminster (Parliament, Big Ben), the West End (theatres, Covent Garden), South Bank (Tate Modern, London Eye), the City of London (financial district, St. Paul's), and Shoreditch (street art, nightlife). Notting Hill, Camden, and Greenwich are great day trips within the city.

The Tube (London Underground) is the primary transit system with 11 lines. Use a contactless bank card or Oyster card — single cash fares are much more expensive. Buses are £1.75 flat fare. The Elizabeth Line connects Heathrow to central London in about 35 minutes for around £12.

Tipping 10-12.5% at restaurants is customary. Check if service charge is already included on the bill. Pubs operate on a no-tipping, order-at-the-bar system. Queuing (standing in line) is taken seriously — never skip a queue.

Spring (April-May) and early autumn (September-October) are ideal. Summer (June-August) brings the longest days and outdoor festivals. Winter is dark and cold but has excellent Christmas markets. London weather is unpredictable — always carry a layer and small umbrella.

Many world-class museums are free: the British Museum, National Gallery, Tate Modern, Natural History Museum, V&A, and Science Museum. Borough Market offers excellent street food for £5-12. Pubs serve meals for £10-18.
