# Paris, France

Paris is divided into 20 arrondissements spiraling outward from the center. The best neighborhoods for first-time visitors are Le Marais (3rd-4th), Saint-Germain-des-Prés (6th), and Montmartre (18th). Avoid the area directly around Gare du Nord at night.

The Paris Métro is the fastest way to get around with 16 lines covering the entire city. A carnet of 10 tickets (t+ tickets) costs about €16.90. The RER B connects Charles de Gaulle airport to central Paris in 35 minutes for about €11.

Tipping in Paris is not required as service is included in the bill (service compris), but leaving 5-10% for good service is appreciated. Always greet shopkeepers with "Bonjour" before asking for anything — skipping this is considered very rude.

Peak tourist season is June through August. Spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) offer milder weather and shorter lines. The Eiffel Tower requires advance booking, especially for summit access. The Louvre is free on the first Saturday evening of each month.

Budget travelers should explore the Canal Saint-Martin area for affordable dining. A typical café lunch (plat du jour) costs €12-18. Bakeries offer excellent sandwiches for €4-6.
