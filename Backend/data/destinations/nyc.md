# New York City, USA

Manhattan is the main tourist borough, organized on a grid system above 14th Street. Key neighborhoods: Midtown (Times Square, Empire State Building), Lower Manhattan (Financial District, 9/11 Memorial), Greenwich Village, SoHo, and the Upper East/West Sides. Brooklyn offers Williamsburg, DUMBO, and Park Slope.

The NYC subway runs 24/7 and costs $2.90 per ride with an OMNY tap-to-pay system or MetroCard. Express trains skip local stops — check the map carefully. Uber and Lyft are available but expensive during rush hour. Walking is often faster for short distances in Manhattan.

Tipping is essential: 18-20% at restaurants, $1-2 per drink at bars, 15-20% for taxi drivers. Tax (8.875%) is added at checkout and not included in displayed prices. Many restaurants are cash-only in Chinatown and parts of the East Village.

Fall (September-November) is the best time to visit with mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is hot and humid (30-35°C). Winter (December-February) is cold but festive. Book Broadway shows through the TKTS booth in Times Square for same-day discounts of 20-50%.

Dollar slice pizza shops offer $1-1.50 slices throughout Manhattan. Food halls like Chelsea Market, Urbanspace, and Gotham West Market offer diverse options for $10-18 per meal.
