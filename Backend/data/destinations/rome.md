# Rome, Italy

Rome's historic center is walkable and concentrated. The main areas are Centro Storico (Pantheon, Piazza Navona), Trastevere (nightlife, authentic dining), Monti (trendy boutiques), and Vatican City (St. Peter's, Sistine Chapel). The Colosseum, Forum, and Palatine Hill are in the archaeological zone south of Centro Storico.

Rome has two metro lines (A and B) plus extensive bus routes. Single tickets cost €1.50 and last 100 minutes. The Roma Pass (€32 for 48h) includes transit and museum entry. Taxis are metered — only use white licensed taxis from official stands.

Dress codes are enforced at the Vatican and major churches: no bare shoulders or shorts above the knee. Carry a scarf or light cardigan. The Vatican Museums are closed on Sundays except the last Sunday of the month (free but extremely crowded).

Spring (April-May) and fall (September-October) are ideal. August is brutally hot and many local restaurants close for summer holiday (Ferragosto). Winter is mild but rainy.

Avoid restaurants with picture menus directly facing major tourist sites — they tend to be tourist traps. Head one or two streets back for authentic trattorias. Aperitivo (pre-dinner drinks with free snacks) typically runs 6-9 PM and is excellent value at €8-12 per drink.
