# Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo is organized around its JR Yamanote Line loop, which connects major hubs: Shinjuku (entertainment), Shibuya (shopping), Akihabara (electronics/anime), Ueno (museums/parks), and Tokyo Station (transit hub). Each neighborhood has a distinct character.

Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card for seamless travel on trains, metros, and buses. A 7-day Japan Rail Pass is worth it only if traveling outside Tokyo. The subway runs approximately 5:00 AM to midnight.

Shoes must be removed when entering homes, many traditional restaurants, and temples — look for shoe racks or a raised floor (genkan). Bowing is the standard greeting; a slight 15-degree bow is appropriate for casual situations. Tipping is not practiced and can be considered offensive.

Cherry blossom season (late March to mid-April) and autumn foliage (November) are peak times. Summer (July-August) is hot and humid with temperatures above 35°C. Typhoon season runs August through October.

Convenience stores (konbini) like 7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart serve surprisingly high-quality meals for ¥300-800. Ramen shops average ¥800-1200. Department store basement floors (depachika) offer premium food at reasonable prices.
